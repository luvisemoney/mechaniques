Revision Notes

Components

Resolve each force into signed x, y, and z components before summing. If the reference angle changes, redraw the triangle instead of guessing sine or cosine.

Resultants

R = ΣF. In 2D, calculate Rx and Ry first, then magnitude and quadrant. “Vertical resultant” means ΣFx = 0.

Moments

Use MO = r × F. The position vector begins at O and ends on the force line. In 2D, use perpendicular distance with a visible sign convention.

Couples

A couple is a free moment M = Fd. Add pure couples directly to the moment resultant, regardless of their location.

3D unit vectors

For a force along AB, form rAB = B − A, calculate |rAB|, divide to get a unit vector, then multiply by the force magnitude.

Submission check

Check units, signs, calculator mode, quadrant, reasonable magnitude, and whether every requested quantity is boxed with units.

Equation Guide

Use this when an equation looks unfamiliar. Read the “means” sentence first, then identify the symbols and units.

Force components

Fx = F cos θ
Fy = F sin θ

Means: split one angled force into horizontal and vertical parts.

  • F: force magnitude, in N
  • θ: angle measured from the chosen axis
  • Fx, Fy: signed components

Resultant force

Rx = ΣFx
Ry = ΣFy
|R| = √(Rx² + Ry²)

Means: add all components first, then calculate the resultant size.

  • Σ: sum of every contribution
  • R: one force equivalent to the system
  • |R|: magnitude of the resultant

Moment about a point

MO = r × F
M = Fd

Means: measure how strongly a force tends to rotate an object about O.

  • O: moment reference point
  • r: vector from O to the force line
  • d: perpendicular distance to the force line
  • ×: cross product; it gives a moment vector

Two-point 3D force

rAB = B − A
uAB = rAB / |rAB|
F = FuAB

Means: build a direction from A to B, normalize it, and scale it.

  • A, B: coordinate points
  • |rAB|: distance from A to B
  • uAB: unit vector; magnitude 1

Dot product and projection

A · B = |A||B| cos θ
Fline = F · u

Means: find an angle or the signed part of a vector along a direction.

  • ·: dot product; result is a scalar
  • u: unit direction vector
  • Fline: component along the line

Moment about an axis

Maxis = uaxis · (r × F)

Means: calculate the full moment, then keep only the part rotating about the chosen axis.

  • uaxis: unit vector along the axis
  • Maxis: scalar moment about that axis

Equilibrium conditions

ΣF = 0
ΣMO = 0

Means: no net translation and no net rotation. Use these only when the problem states equilibrium or asks for reactions.

  • ΣF: net force vector
  • ΣMO: net moment about O
  • 0: zero vector or zero scalar

Mechanics symbols & acronyms

Search for notation from the equations, slides, and quiz. For example, Σ means “add every listed contribution,” while FBD means free-body diagram.
